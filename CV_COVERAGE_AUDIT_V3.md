## Editorial boundaries

- `$2.55M` is the ARR associated with the RingCentral customer; no individual revenue-creation claim.
- `22%` Tier-2 and `30%` ordering changes were reported by Business/Product stakeholders; no invented measurement methodology.
- `₹1.5Cr+` is net-new contract revenue for Ambee's broader environmental product portfolio, not for the gateway alone.
- `10M+ API calls` and `20M+ tile requests` are two different services; `20K+ sessions` belongs to Climate Maps.
- `1,200+` denotes physical devices and weather stations, **not** software integrations; `>99.5%` is reported platform uptime.
- Shared SSO is **one** capability serving enterprise and academic access; not two implementations.
- The cross-company device story does **not** imply a single system, engineering ownership, or identical responsibilities at every employer.
- Graphics are conceptual diagrams, not employer-provided UI screenshots.
