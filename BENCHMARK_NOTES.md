## Accuracy principles for visuals

- The graphics are **explanatory, not evidence of private implementations**. The labels reflect only capabilities and relationships supported by the CV. They are not technical architecture diagrams, wireframes, performance charts, or screenshots.
- No measured business performance is illustrated as a statistically precise chart; no invented baseline or change-over-time series appears.
- No hidden employer/customer names, internal metrics, logos, or unauthorized product screens are used.
- The visualizer avoids claiming that multi-user provisioning was fixed, that all support queries resolved automatically, or that academia and the API dashboard had separate SSO implementations.
- Course images come from the user's screenshot, and do not include credential verification URLs. Add links only after verification.
